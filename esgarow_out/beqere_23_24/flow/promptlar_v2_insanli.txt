=== KLIP 01 · səs 01 · "Onun bənzəri bir surə gətirin!… 14 əsrdir heç kim…" ===
Vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts. Cinematic photorealistic film look, 7th-century Arabian desert at night. The famous poetry fair of Ukaz: tents of dark wool lit by torches and oil lamps, a crowd of Arab men in traditional robes and turbans sits on rugs in a circle. In the middle, a proud poet with a long black beard stands on a low rock, reciting passionately with raised arm; the crowd listens in awe, firelight flickering on their faces. Slow camera push-in through the crowd toward the poet. Warm amber firelight, deep blue night, embers drifting. Realistic, dignified. No text.
